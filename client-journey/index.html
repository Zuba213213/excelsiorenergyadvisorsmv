Client Journey

← Back to Vault

Six stages, first discovery to referral — each one shaping what the website has to do at that moment.

Stage 1

Discovery

A referral, a community event, or a search for "is my electric rate good" brings someone to the site for the first time — usually skeptical, often burned before by an energy salesperson. Site's job: prove trustworthiness and the "free to you" model in the first screen, before asking for anything.

Stage 2

Consultation

The client shares their current bill or usage — low-friction, no pressure, in the language they're comfortable in. Site's job: make this step feel like handing something to a trusted person, not submitting a lead form to a call center.

Stage 3

Comparison & Quote

Joe (or the Excelsior team) shops the market and comes back with real options, explained plainly, with the "why" behind the recommendation. Site's job: set the expectation that this comes from a person with 20+ years in the industry, not an algorithm.

Stage 4

Enrollment

The client picks a plan and Excelsior handles the switch. Site's job: reassure that there's no cost, no catch, and Excelsior stays the point of contact if anything goes wrong with the provider.

Stage 5

Onboarding & Ongoing

The switch is confirmed, the client starts seeing the new rate on their bill. For commercial and Tier 3 accounts, Excelsior tracks the renewal date proactively. Site's job: introduce the idea that Excelsior can also shop cable, internet, and other services — the horizontal expansion, timed for when trust is highest.

Stage 6

Referral

A satisfied client — especially in a tight-knit community — refers a neighbor, family member, or fellow business owner. Site's job: make referring effortless (a shareable link, a simple "send this to someone" moment), since word-of-mouth is the strongest trust signal this audience responds to.
