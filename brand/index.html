Who Excelsior Is

A veteran-led brokerage that finds Texas families and businesses the most affordable energy, cable, internet, and financial services available to them — without the door-to-door pressure the deregulated market is known for.

Excelsior Energy Advisors is led by Joe Zubillaga, who brings 20+ years in the energy industry, including experience at Vistra Corp, one of the largest power generators and retail electricity providers in Texas. That background sits on the inside of an industry most households only ever meet through a knock at the door or a confusing bill — and it's the reason Excelsior exists as the outside advocate instead.

The positioning statement

Excelsior Energy Advisors is the independent broker who already knows how the deregulated energy market works — and uses that knowledge on the client's side of the table, not the provider's.

The Model: Brokerage, Not Provider

Excelsior doesn't generate or sell energy — it shops the market on the client's behalf and recommends the most affordable, legitimate plan for their actual usage. This is the standard energy-broker structure: Excelsior is compensated by the provider the client ultimately signs with, not by a fee charged to the client. The service itself costs the client nothing.

That "free to you" model only works as a trust story if it's said plainly and repeated everywhere — on the site, in conversation, in every piece of collateral. It's the single fact most likely to overcome the skepticism a market full of predatory door-to-door sales has trained people to feel.

Why This Matters to the Hispanic Texas Market Specifically

Hispanic households and small businesses in deregulated Texas markets are disproportionately targeted by aggressive, sometimes deceptive, retail energy sales tactics — often in-language, often door-to-door, often engineered to make comparison shopping feel impossible. Excelsior's positioning is built directly against that pattern: a bilingual, community-rooted advisor who does the comparison shopping the market was designed to discourage.

Service Area

Texas first, with room to grow. Excelsior starts inside Texas's deregulated electricity market and is built to expand into other deregulated states as the client base grows.

The Horizontal Expansion

Energy is the entry point, not the ceiling. Once a client trusts Excelsior to find their best electricity rate, the same brokerage relationship extends naturally to the other recurring household and business bills people overpay for:

  • Energy — the founding service, built on Joe's direct industry background
  • Cable — comparison shopping for TV/cable packages
  • Internet — comparison shopping for home and business internet plans
  • Financial services — expansion into related advisory/comparison services
  • And more — the model generalizes to any recurring service where shopping around saves real money

Every section of this vault should be written with that horizontal roadmap in mind — the brand needs room to say "energy" today and "energy, cable, internet, and more" within the year without a rebrand.
